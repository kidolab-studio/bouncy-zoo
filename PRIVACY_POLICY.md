## English

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026  
**App:** Bouncy Zoo  
**Publisher:** Emir Salih Gümrük (KidoLab)  
**Contact:** emirsalihgumruk@gmail.com

Bouncy Zoo is an offline educational mini-game application designed for children ages 4–8.

### Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, age, precise geolocation, device identifiers, photos, camera data, or microphone data. It contains no user accounts, authentication, advertisements, analytics, crash reporting, social media integrations, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet or transmit data to external servers.

### Information stored on the device

Game progress, high scores, audio preferences, and Coloring Book artwork are stored strictly within the app's local device storage (`user://`). This data is never sent to the publisher or shared with other parties. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage will permanently delete these local records.

### Children’s privacy (COPPA and Families Policy)

The app does not solicit or collect personal data from children. Parents are encouraged to review store settings, device permissions, and local storage. If any future update incorporates data-processing features, this policy and store declarations will be revised accordingly.

### Third parties and external links

The current version establishes no connections to third-party servers or external websites. Open-source licenses and attribution addresses are displayed as plain text only and cannot be opened via external links within the app.

### Your rights and contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Emir Salih Gümrük at emirsalihgumruk@gmail.com. Because the app does not process personal data online, there is no server-side account to access, rectify, or delete; local records can be cleared directly through your device settings.

This document serves as an informational policy compliant with Google Play Families, COPPA, and GDPR requirements.
